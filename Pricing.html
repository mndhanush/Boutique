Subscription FAQ

Can I customize my subscription flowers?

Yes. While our Classic Bloom tier relies on the florist's seasonal choice, our Premium Luxe members can set color palettes and exclude specific flowers (e.g., lilies if you have pets).

How do you handle event retainers?

A non-refundable $1,000 deposit is required to secure your date in our calendar. This acts as a retainer and is fully credited toward your final event invoice.

Are vases included with deliveries?

Your first delivery will arrive with a complimentary premium glass vase. Subsequent deliveries are wrapped in eco-friendly hydration packs to easily drop into your existing vase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

There are no long-term lock-ins. You can cancel your subscription at any time via your account portal, as long as it is 48 hours before your next scheduled delivery.
